What this is

A working model of what an international asset oversight team monitors each quarter, rebuilt from Hibiscus Petroleum's public Corporate and Business Updates. It tracks each producing asset's production, uptime, unit cost, realised price and sales against the company's own plan, explains movements in management's words, flags data that changed after publication, and tests what the 2030 Mission requires of the reserve base.
