# Asset Oversight Portal: Hibiscus Petroleum

An outside-in asset oversight tracker built from Hibiscus Petroleum's public quarterly Corporate and Business Updates (FY2025 to FY2026): production, uptime, unit cost, realised price, sales against the company's own plan, reserves and the 2030 Mission bridge.

Built by Samuel Aaron Miranda. Not affiliated with Hibiscus Petroleum Berhad. All figures come from public company disclosures; USD revenue and cost figures are estimates.
